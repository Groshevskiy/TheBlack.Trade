## 1. Purpose

This pack defines the required customer-facing legal and disclosure materials for TheBlack.Trade, including terms of service, privacy notice, fee and FX disclosures, risk warnings, cancellation/refund wording, receipt language and other mandatory communication artifacts.

## 2. Minimum components

The pack should contain terms of service, privacy notice, fee disclosure model, quote/FX explanation language, AML/KYC disclosure, risk notice, complaint/escalation notice and regulated receipt/disclaimer language.


## 3. Controlled document set

The pack should maintain authoritative customer-facing variants for:

- terms of service;
- privacy notice;
- fee and FX disclosure;
- risk disclosures for fiat-to-crypto flows;
- complaint and escalation notice;
- receipt footer and transactional disclaimer language.

## 4. Governance and approvals

Each controlled document should have an owner, approval route, version, effective date, locale coverage and change trigger. Product copy must not diverge from approved legal wording where regulated meaning is involved.

## 5. Display and delivery obligations

The spec should identify where each disclosure must appear: registration, quote flow, order confirmation, payment instructions, receipts, payout confirmations, help center and customer support macros.

## 6. Localization and evidence

Every localized disclosure should be traceable to a master approved source, with records of review, publication date and retirement when superseded.
